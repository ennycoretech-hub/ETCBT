CBT Examination

Read Before You Begin

General

  1. This is a Computer-Based Test (CBT) simulating the JAMB standard format.
  2. Your exam will contain English Language (60 questions) plus three other subjects (40 questions each) — 180 questions in total.
  3. You will have a fixed amount of time to complete the entire exam. The countdown timer is shown at the top of the exam screen and cannot be paused or extended.
  4. When the timer reaches zero, your exam is automatically submitted with whatever answers you have selected at that point.

Navigation

  • Use Next and Previous to move between questions, or open the sidebar to jump directly to any question number.
  • Answered questions are colored blue in the sidebar; unanswered ones remain uncolored why current quest have a white outline.
  • You may change your answer to any question at any time before final submission.
  • You may also double click the sidebar or calculator to hide its display.
  • Keyboard shortcuts are available: A–D to select an option, N/S for next, P for previous, and Ctrl+Enter to submit.

Conduct During the Exam

  • Do not switch tabs, minimize the browser, or leave the exam window. Repeated tab-switching will terminate your session.
  • Do not copy, paste, or right-click during the exam — these actions are disabled and logged.
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection before starting. A network status banner will alert you if you go offline.
  • All actions during the exam are logged. Any detected violation may result in automatic termination and a recorded scores of zero.

Submission

  • You may submit early using the Submit button once you are satisfied with your answers
  • After submission, you will confirm your details on a summary screen before your result is sent for final recording.
  • Ensure you have a stable internet connection before submission. A network status banner will alert you if you go of banner.
  • Once submitted, your answers cannot be changed.

You must accept the terms above to continue.
